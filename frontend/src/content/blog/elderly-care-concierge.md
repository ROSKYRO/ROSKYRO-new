# Elderly Care Concierge: Ongoing Support Beyond a Single Visit

![Two Indian ROSKYRO Partners in white ROSKYRO t-shirts helping a senior with an appointment calendar](/blog/images/elderly-care-concierge.svg "ROSKYRO Elderly Care Concierge – ongoing senior support")

Caring for an ageing parent is rarely a single task — it's a recurring pattern of appointments, prescriptions to track, and hospital visits that need to be planned around. **Elderly Care Concierge** is built for that pattern: ongoing, coordinated support rather than a one-time booking, with a Partner who understands the parent's routine well enough to actually be useful over time.

## What is Elderly Care Concierge?

This is ROSKYRO's continuity-focused service for seniors — a Partner who accompanies hospital visits, manages the appointment calendar, and provides general day-to-day assistance on an ongoing basis. Unlike a single hospital visit, this service is designed around a relationship that builds over weeks and months.

## Who is this for?

- Adult children coordinating a parent's care from another city
- Seniors managing multiple ongoing conditions with regular specialist visits
- Families who've tried one-off bookings and want a more consistent arrangement
- Anyone who wants a single Partner who already knows their parent's history, medications, and preferences

## How it works

1. **Set up Elderly Care Concierge** and share your parent's regular care needs — upcoming appointments, known conditions, preferences.
2. **Your concierge Partner builds familiarity** with the routine over the first few visits.
3. **Ongoing coordination** — accompanying appointments, tracking what's next, and handling the small logistics that pile up.
4. **Regular updates to the family**, so distant relatives stay informed without having to ask.
5. **Hourly billing** with a live estimate for each visit, tracked via Start and End PINs.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

The difference between a one-time hospital companion and a concierge who's been with your parent for three months isn't small — it's the difference between explaining the situation every single time and having someone who already knows. That continuity is what actually reduces the mental load on the family member managing everything from a distance.

## Pricing

Elderly Care Concierge is billed hourly, with each visit estimated live in the app based on expected duration.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is this different from Elder Companion Care?**
Elder Companion Care focuses on daily companionship and light support at home. Elderly Care Concierge is built around ongoing coordination of appointments and hospital visits over time — more structured, less about company and more about managing recurring care needs.

**Can I get updates after each visit?**
Yes, families typically receive updates after appointments so they stay informed without needing to call.

**Does the same Partner return for each visit?**
The concierge model is designed for continuity so the Partner builds familiarity with your parent's routine and history.

**What if my parent has multiple specialists to see in a month?**
That's exactly the pattern this service is built for — coordinating across multiple appointments rather than booking each one separately.
