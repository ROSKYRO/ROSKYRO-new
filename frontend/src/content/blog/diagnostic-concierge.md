# Diagnostic Concierge: One Less Errand Between You and Your Reports

![An Indian ROSKYRO Partner in a white ROSKYRO t-shirt collecting a diagnostic report at a test centre](/blog/images/diagnostic-concierge.svg "ROSKYRO Diagnostic Concierge – test booking to report collection")

Getting a diagnostic test done sounds simple until it involves booking a slot, travelling to the centre, sometimes travelling back for the report, and doing it all again for a follow-up test. **Diagnostic Concierge** takes that string of small errands off a family's plate — booking, coordination, and collection, handled end to end.

## What is Diagnostic Concierge?

Diagnostic Concierge is ROSKYRO's service for managing the full diagnostic-test process: booking the test, coordinating with the diagnostic centre, and collecting or delivering the report once it's ready. It's designed for the routine but time-consuming errands that add up, especially for families managing tests for an elderly relative.

## Who is this for?

- Families booking routine or follow-up diagnostic tests for elderly parents
- Working professionals who don't have time to personally visit a diagnostic centre during work hours
- Patients who need a test done but can't easily travel to the centre themselves
- Anyone managing multiple tests across different centres who wants one person coordinating all of it

## How it works

1. **Book Diagnostic Concierge** and specify the test(s) needed and any centre preference.
2. **The Partner coordinates the booking** with the diagnostic centre, confirming timing and any preparation instructions.
3. **Sample collection or centre visit** is coordinated on the scheduled day.
4. **Report collection**, once ready, either picked up from the centre or coordinated for delivery.
5. **Reports are handed off to the family**, closing out the booking.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

It's rarely one test — it's a panel of tests, sometimes at more than one centre, sometimes with a follow-up needed a week later. A Diagnostic Concierge means the family isn't the one tracking which report is ready and which centre still needs a visit. That coordination, done consistently, is worth more than the individual errands it replaces.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the Partner interpret or explain the test results?**
No — the Partner coordinates booking, sample collection logistics, and report delivery. Interpretation of results should come from your doctor.

**Can I book multiple tests across different diagnostic centres in one request?**
Yes, share all the tests and centres needed and the Partner coordinates across them.

**How do I receive the report once it's collected?**
Reports are handed off to the family as agreed at booking — in person, or coordinated for delivery, depending on what you need.

**Is home sample collection included?**
Coordination for home sample collection can be arranged where the diagnostic centre supports it; confirm availability for your specific test when booking.
