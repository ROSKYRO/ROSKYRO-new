# Hospital Concierge: One Point of Contact from Admission to Discharge

![A ROSKYRO Hospital Concierge Partner in a white ROSKYRO polo showing a tablet to an elderly couple at the hospital reception](/blog/images/hospital-concierge-2.png "ROSKYRO Hospital Concierge – One Point of Contact from Admission to Discharge")

A hospital stay rarely involves just one visit — it's admission paperwork on day one, floor-level coordination through the stay, and a discharge process on the way out that somehow always takes longer than expected. **Hospital Concierge** gives a family a single, dedicated point of contact for that entire journey, instead of starting from scratch with hospital staff at every stage.

## What is Hospital Concierge?

Hospital Concierge is ROSKYRO's end-to-end coordination service for a hospital stay. A dedicated Partner manages admission formalities, coordinates attendant support on the hospital floor throughout the stay, and handles the discharge process — so the family has one person to call, not five different departments.

## Who is this for?

- Families admitting a relative for a planned procedure or extended stay
- Anyone who has experienced how fragmented hospital coordination usually is and wants a single owner for the process
- Working professionals who can't be physically present at the hospital throughout a multi-day stay
- Families managing a hospital admission for a parent while also managing their own work and household

## How it works

1. **Book Hospital Concierge** and share the hospital, expected admission date, and any known procedure details.
2. **Your concierge Partner coordinates admission** — paperwork, formalities, and initial floor arrangements.
3. **Ongoing coordination through the stay**, including attendant support and updates to the family.
4. **Discharge management** — collecting reports, completing formalities, and coordinating the transition out of the hospital.
5. **Billing is hourly and tracked** with Start and End PINs across the engagement.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

Hospital systems are built around departments, not around families. A concierge model flips that — one Partner who already understands the situation from admission day doesn't need to be re-briefed at discharge. That continuity alone removes a significant amount of the stress that usually falls on whichever family member happens to be free that day.

## Frequently asked questions

**How is this different from Hospital Relationship Officer?**
Hospital Relationship Officer is built for single OPD visits or short admission support. Hospital Concierge covers the full arc of a hospital stay — admission through discharge — with one continuous point of contact.

**Will the same Partner be with us for the whole stay?**
The concierge model is designed for continuity; the app will confirm Partner assignment and any handovers as part of your booking.

**Can the concierge speak directly with hospital staff on our behalf?**
Yes, coordinating with hospital staff on formalities and updates is core to what a Hospital Concierge does.

**What happens if the discharge date changes?**
You can update the booking as the stay progresses — billing reflects actual time worked, not the original estimate.
