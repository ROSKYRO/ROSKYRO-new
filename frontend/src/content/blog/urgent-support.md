Emergencies don't check the clock. A parent falls at 2 a.m. and needs someone at the house before an ambulance arrives. A relative lands at the airport at midnight with nowhere to go. A family member is stuck at a hospital counter at 11 p.m. with no one else around. **24x7 Urgent Support** is ROSKYRO's round-the-clock service for exactly these moments — non-medical, fast, and available any hour of any day.

## What is 24x7 Urgent Support?

This service connects you with a verified ROSKYRO Partner for urgent, non-medical assistance whenever it's needed — not just during standard daytime hours. It's built for the situations that can't wait for a next-day appointment: being physically present, coordinating with hospital staff, or simply providing a trusted pair of hands until the situation is stable.

## Who is this for?

- Families dealing with a sudden situation involving an elderly or unwell relative
- Anyone needing someone present at odd hours when other family members can't get there in time
- People coordinating care for a relative in another city, needing "boots on the ground" immediately
- Situations where the need is urgent but not a medical emergency requiring an ambulance or doctor

## How it works

1. **Open the ROSKYRO app** and select 24x7 Urgent Support — no advance scheduling required.
2. **Share the address and situation** so the Partner arrives prepared for what's needed.
3. **A nearby Partner is dispatched**, arriving in a white ROSKYRO t-shirt with verified ID.
4. **Support begins immediately**, tracked with a Start PIN the moment help starts.
5. **Billing closes with an End PIN**, reflecting only the time actually spent.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

The value of urgent support isn't the hourly rate — it's the fact that it exists at all, at 3 a.m., on a Sunday, on a festival day, whenever it's needed. Because every Partner is background-verified in advance (not vetted in a panic, in the moment), families can call for help without a second layer of worry about who is showing up.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this a replacement for calling an ambulance in a medical emergency?**
No. For a medical emergency, always call emergency medical services first. 24x7 Urgent Support is for non-medical assistance — presence, coordination, and logistics — alongside or after emergency care.

**How quickly can a Partner reach me?**
Response time depends on Partner availability in your area at that moment; the app shows real-time status when you request support.

**Can I book this for someone else, like a parent in a different city?**
Yes — enter their address and add a contact-on-arrival name and number so the Partner knows exactly who to look for.

**Is the rate different from daytime services?**
Yes, 24x7 Urgent Support has its own hourly rate reflecting round-the-clock, on-demand availability — shown clearly before you confirm a booking.
