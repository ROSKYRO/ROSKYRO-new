# Medical Travel Concierge: End-to-End Support for Outstation Treatment

![An Indian ROSKYRO Partner in a white ROSKYRO t-shirt helping an outstation patient with travel and hospital coordination](/blog/images/medical-travel-concierge.svg "ROSKYRO Medical Travel Concierge – outstation patient support")

Travelling to a different city for treatment adds an entire layer of logistics on top of an already stressful situation — unfamiliar roads, an unfamiliar hospital, and no one local to turn to if something goes wrong. **Medical Travel Concierge** covers that whole journey: from the moment a patient arrives in the city to the moment they head home.

## What is Medical Travel Concierge?

This is ROSKYRO's most comprehensive service, coordinating every stage of an outstation patient's journey — arrival in the city, transport to the hospital, stay arrangements, day-of-treatment support, and the return journey home. It's designed for patients and families who don't have a local network to lean on.

## Who is this for?

- Patients travelling from smaller towns to cities for specialised treatment
- Families sending an elderly relative for treatment without being able to accompany them the whole way
- NRI or out-of-state families coordinating a parent's treatment in India
- Anyone who needs a reliable local presence in an unfamiliar city during a medical trip

## How it works

1. **Book Medical Travel Concierge** with details of the arrival date, hospital, and expected length of stay.
2. **Arrival coordination** — a Partner meets the patient and manages transport to the hospital or accommodation.
3. **Stay arrangements** are coordinated so the patient has a place to be between treatment days.
4. **Treatment-day support** — accompanying appointments, procedures, or admission as needed.
5. **Return journey coordination**, closing out the engagement once treatment is complete.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

The biggest risk in outstation treatment isn't the treatment itself — it's everything around it: a missed connection, an unfamiliar hospital layout, a patient alone in a city they don't know. A single concierge who owns the whole journey removes that risk, giving the family in another city a reliable way to know their relative is looked after at every stage, not just during the actual appointment.

## Pricing

Given the scope of coordination involved, Medical Travel Concierge carries ROSKYRO's highest hourly rate; the app provides a detailed live estimate before booking, covering the full engagement.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does this include booking my hospital appointment itself?**
Medical Travel Concierge coordinates travel, stay, and treatment-day logistics around your appointment; hospital appointment booking should be confirmed with the hospital, and the concierge coordinates around that schedule.

**Can this be booked for a parent travelling alone?**
Yes — this is one of the primary use cases. Share the parent's travel details and the Partner coordinates the full journey on their behalf.

**What if the treatment takes longer than planned?**
The engagement can be extended, and billing reflects actual time and coordination provided.

**Is accommodation included in the price?**
The concierge coordinates stay arrangements; accommodation costs themselves are separate from the ROSKYRO service fee, which is shown clearly in your estimate.
