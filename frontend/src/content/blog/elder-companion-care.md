# Elder Companion Care: Reassuring, Everyday Support for Ageing Parents

![A ROSKYRO Elder Companion Care Partner in a white ROSKYRO polo sitting with an elderly man at home, hand on his shoulder](/blog/images/elder-companion-care.png "ROSKYRO Elder Companion Care – Reassuring, Everyday Support for Ageing Parents")

For many Indian families, the hardest part of ageing parents living alone isn't a medical emergency — it's the ordinary Tuesday afternoon with no one to talk to, no one to remind them to eat on time, and no one to notice if something feels off. **Elder Companion Care** exists for exactly that gap: everyday companionship and light support, delivered by a trained Partner who shows up consistently.

## What is Elder Companion Care?

This is an hourly companionship service where a verified ROSKYRO Partner spends time with an elderly family member — conversation, light assistance around the house, accompanying them for a walk or errand, and simply being a reassuring presence. It isn't clinical caregiving; it's the kind of support that keeps daily life steady and reduces the isolation that so often affects seniors living apart from their children.

## Who is this for?

- Adult children living in another city (or country) whose parents live alone
- Seniors who are physically fine but would benefit from regular company
- Families wanting a trial run before committing to a full-time attendant
- Anyone who wants a trusted, background-verified person checking in on a parent between their own visits

## How it works

1. **Book Elder Companion Care** through the app and set the hours you'd like — a single visit or a recurring schedule.
2. **Share context** about your parent's routine, preferences, and anything the Partner should know.
3. **The Partner arrives** in a white ROSKYRO t-shirt with ID, and the visit begins with a Start PIN.
4. **Time together** — conversation, a walk, help with small daily tasks, or simply company during meals.
5. **Visit ends with an End PIN**, and you're billed for the time actually spent.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

Trust is the whole product here. Every Partner is background-verified before they're allowed near a family's home, and the Start/End PIN system means the family always knows exactly when a visit began and ended — no ambiguity, no unease about a stranger's access. For families managing care from a distance, this is often the difference between constant worry and a manageable routine.

## Pricing

Elder Companion Care is billed hourly. The app shows a live estimate — including any arrival or return fee — before you confirm the booking.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I set up a recurring schedule instead of booking one visit at a time?**
Yes, many families use Elder Companion Care as a regular weekly or daily arrangement rather than a one-off booking.

**Is this a substitute for a full-time caregiver or nurse?**
No — it's companionship and light daily support, not clinical or round-the-clock caregiving. For hospital-related needs, see ROSKYRO's Elderly Care Concierge or Hospital Concierge services.

**How do I know who is coming to my parent's home?**
Every booking confirms the Partner's identity in advance, and Partners are background-verified and wear ROSKYRO-branded uniforms for easy recognition.

**What if my parent needs help with an errand outside the house?**
That's within scope — Partners can accompany seniors for walks, errands, or short outings as part of the booking.
