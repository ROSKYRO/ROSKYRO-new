# Hospital Relationship Officer: A Trained Companion for OPD Visits and Admission Days

![A ROSKYRO Hospital Relationship Officer Partner in a white ROSKYRO polo with lanyard, smiling with an elderly patient near the OPD registration desk](/blog/images/hospital-relationship-officer.png "ROSKYRO Hospital Relationship Officer – A Trained Companion for OPD Visits and Admission Days")

Anyone who has spent a morning in an OPD queue knows the drill: a token number, three different counters, a form that needs a signature you don't have, and a family member who has taken a day off work just to stand in line. **Hospital Relationship Officer** is ROSKYRO's answer to that exact morning — a trained, background-verified Partner who shows up at the hospital with you (or for you) and handles the parts of a hospital visit that have nothing to do with medicine and everything to do with logistics.

## What is Hospital Relationship Officer?

Hospital Relationship Officer is an hourly, on-demand service where a ROSKYRO Partner accompanies a patient or family member through OPD visits, admission formalities, and general hospital-floor support. Every Partner arrives in a white ROSKYRO t-shirt with the company logo, carries verified ID, and is trained specifically for hospital environments — queue navigation, paperwork, and being a calm, reliable second pair of hands when a family is stretched thin.

## Who books this service?

- Working professionals who can't take a full day off for a routine OPD visit
- Families whose elderly parents need someone with them at the hospital, but not necessarily a medical attendant
- Patients travelling alone for a consultation who want a local point of contact
- Anyone facing an admission process with paperwork they'd rather not navigate solo

## How it works

1. **Choose Hospital Relationship Officer** on the ROSKYRO app and enter the hours you expect to need.
2. **Share the visit details** — hospital name, appointment time, and any specific paperwork or department involved.
3. **Meet your Partner** at the hospital at the scheduled time; they check in using a Start PIN so billing begins exactly when help starts.
4. **Get support through the visit** — queue management, form filling, liaising with hospital staff, and general assistance.
5. **Close out with an End PIN** — you're billed only for the actual time worked, not the time you booked.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

Hospital corridors are confusing even when you're not worried about someone you love. A Hospital Relationship Officer Partner removes the friction — the queue-jumping frustration, the "which counter is this form for" moments — so the family member who does show up can focus on the patient instead of the process. Because billing is based on actual time (Start PIN to End PIN), there's no incentive to drag out a booking, and no surprise if the visit finishes early.

## Pricing

Hospital Relationship Officer is billed hourly, with the exact rate and any arrival/return fees shown as a live estimate before you confirm the booking in the app.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the Partner medically trained?**
Hospital Relationship Officer Partners are trained for non-medical, logistical support — queues, paperwork, and coordination — not clinical care. For medical escort or nursing needs, ask about ROSKYRO's other concierge services.

**Can I book Hospital Relationship Officer for a parent who will be alone at the hospital?**
Yes — this is one of the most common uses. You can add a contact-on-arrival name and phone number when booking so your Partner knows exactly who to meet.

**How is billing calculated if the visit runs longer than expected?**
You're charged for actual time worked, tracked between the Start PIN and End PIN, not the hours you originally booked.

**Which cities is this available in?**
ROSKYRO is expanding city by city — check the app for live availability in your area.
