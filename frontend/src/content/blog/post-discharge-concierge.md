# Post-Discharge Concierge: Support for the Days That Come After

![A ROSKYRO Post-Discharge Concierge Partner in a white ROSKYRO polo showing a tablet to an elderly couple near the hospital reception](/blog/images/post-discharge-concierge.png "ROSKYRO Post-Discharge Concierge – Support for the Days That Come After")

Discharge day feels like the finish line, but the days right after often matter just as much as the hospital stay itself — new medications to organise, a follow-up appointment to remember, and a patient who's home but not yet back to normal. **Post-Discharge Concierge** supports exactly this transition, so the recovery period doesn't fall entirely on one exhausted family member.

## What is Post-Discharge Concierge?

This service supports the transition from hospital to home after discharge, including coordinating follow-up appointments and helping the family keep track of the medication and care instructions that come with a discharge summary. It picks up where the hospital's responsibility ends and the family's begins.

## Who is this for?

- Families bringing a relative home after a hospital stay or procedure
- Patients who need help staying on top of follow-up appointments and medication schedules
- Adult children who managed the hospital stay but need support once their relative is home
- Anyone who has experienced how easy it is for post-discharge instructions to fall through the cracks

## How it works

1. **Book Post-Discharge Concierge** around your discharge date and share the discharge summary if available.
2. **Transition support home** — helping settle the patient in and organising what's needed for the first days.
3. **Medication and instruction tracking**, helping the family stay on schedule with what the hospital prescribed.
4. **Follow-up appointment coordination**, so nothing gets missed in the weeks after discharge.
5. **Ongoing check-ins** as needed, billed hourly for time actually spent.

## Why families choose ROSKYRO for this

Hospitals are good at treating the condition that got someone admitted; they're less equipped to manage what happens once a patient is back home juggling five new instructions and a family member who's already exhausted from the hospital stay. A Post-Discharge Concierge closes that gap, turning a discharge summary into an actual, followed plan.

## Pricing

Post-Discharge Concierge is billed hourly, with a live estimate shown in the app based on the support you need in the days following discharge.

## Frequently asked questions

**When should I book this — before or after discharge?**
Booking ahead of the expected discharge date lets the Partner prepare, but it can also be booked reactively once discharge happens.

**Does this include nursing or medical care at home?**
No — this is coordination and logistical support, not clinical nursing care. For medical needs at home, consult your hospital's home-care recommendations.

**Can the Partner help track multiple medications and their timings?**
Yes, helping the family stay organised around medication schedules and instructions is a core part of this service.

**How long does a typical Post-Discharge Concierge engagement last?**
It depends on the recovery — some families need support for a few days, others prefer ongoing check-ins through the first few follow-up appointments. Bookings are flexible and billed for actual time used.
